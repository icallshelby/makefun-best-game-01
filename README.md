# Emberflock: Seven Nights of Light

[![make.fun](https://img.shields.io/badge/make.fun-best--game--01-blue)](https://make.fun/competitions/best-game-01)
[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-yellow.svg)](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT)

An atmospheric boids-flock strategy game built for `best-game-01`. Guide a swarm of glowing ember-moths across seven nights through three distinct realms to illuminate all lamps before dawn.

## Gameplay & Mechanics
- **Swarm Steering:** Move your mouse or touch pointer to lead the ember-moth swarm. Moths exhibit boid behaviors (separation, alignment, cohesion). Press Space or click to trigger a light burst.
- **Seven Nights, Three Realms:**
  - *Meadow* (Nights 1–2): Gentle twilight, learn flock navigation, ignite lamps.
  - *Old Town* (Nights 3–5): Introduce mirror shards reflecting ember beams to unlock shadowed lamps, alongside shadow-wisps.
  - *The Deep* (Nights 6–7): Dim lighting, multiple shadow-wisps hunting stray moths, complex mirror alignment.
- **Victory Condition:** Light all lamps across all 7 nights to witness the Dawn Break.
- **Defeat Condition:** Timer runs out or flock size drops below 12 moths due to shadow-wisp predation.

## Controls
- **Steer:** Mouse cursor / Touch pointer.
- **Burst / Confirm:** Space / Enter / Pointer click.
- **Pause:** P / Escape.
- **Restart:** R.
